# FEAT. 💿
### You know the names. Know the album?

You recognise every feature. You know who produced it. You definitely have a very strong opinion about the tracklist.

Now name the record.

## 🎧 [PLAY FEAT.](https://mr-hipp0.github.io/feat./)

Free to play. No account needed. One suspiciously confident guess away from greatness.

---

## How to play

1. **Read the room.** Start with one third of an album’s collaborators. The main artist is keeping a low profile.
2. **Name that record.** Search for an album, select it, and hit **Guess**.
3. **Call for backup.** A miss reveals more names. After two misses, everyone’s on the guest list.
4. **Protect the streak.** You get three tries. Solve it, add one to your run, and keep digging.

Skip spends a guess. On the final attempt it becomes **Give up** — sometimes the liner notes win.

## Choose your record-store aisle

| Setting | Your move |
| --- | --- |
| **Easy** | The top 50 most-streamed albums matching your filters. Start with the heavy hitters. |
| **Medium** | The top 200, including every Easy album. A little more crate digging. |
| **Hard** | Every matching album, including Easy and Medium. Bring your deep-cut opinions. |
| **Genres** | Find your aisle: hip-hop, pop, R&B, rock, Latin, electronic, or country. |
| **Decades** | Pick one era or mix a few. The 2000s and 2020s can share a shelf. |
| **Extra clues** | Add selected producers and songwriters when the guest list needs reinforcements. |

Difficulty is based on total streams **within your selected filters**. Smaller pools use the albums available.

Changes save automatically and start a fresh matching round. Your streak stays. Sound preferences update without changing the puzzle.

## Built for liner-note nerds

- **557 album records**, with **525 playable using guest artists alone**.
- Album-cover search suggestions, because “the blue one” is a perfectly normal starting point.
- All revealed names shown automatically. No hunting for an expand button.
- Full-screen album reveals, personal streaks, and a saved best score.
- Official Deezer players on supported albums, plus listening links when a preview isn’t available.
- Browser-local saves. Close the tab; your record-store shift can resume later.

Your scores live in the browser you play in. They don’t automatically travel between devices or from localhost to this website.

## A quick word from the liner notes

Credits come mainly from sourced Wikipedia album pages and track listings. Popularity uses a dated public [Kworb streaming reference](https://kworb.net/spotify/albums.html), so this is a curated, stream-ranked catalog rather than a live or certified global chart.

The main album artist and sample-only credits stay out of the clues. Optional producer and songwriter coverage varies by album. The game links its credit sources after the reveal.

Inspired by [HIVEMIND’s “Guess the Album from the Features”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FGeXy8AtL20). An independent fan-made game — powered by a love of music and an unreasonable amount of attention to feature lists.
