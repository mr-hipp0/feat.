    A QUICK SPIN

    Read the names.
    Name the record.

    1. 01
      Start with a third.

      You'll see the first third of an album's collaborators. The main artist stays hidden.

    2. 02
      Search. Select. Guess.

      Pick an album from the suggestions, then hit Guess. A miss reveals another third. You get 3 tries.

    3. 03
      Keep the streak alive.

      Each solved album adds 1. Three misses or giving up end the run; your best streak stays saved.

    Skip uses one try. On your final guess, Give up reveals the answer. Changing difficulty, genre, decades, or credit types starts a matching new puzzle immediately. Names are shuffled once per round.

    THE SMALL PRINT, BUT SMALLER

    Made for music people.

    Inspired by HIVEMIND's “Guess the Album from the Features.” An independent game with a sourced album catalog and no account required.

    How credits work

    Verified guest performances can count even when they aren't in a track title. Selected producers and direct songwriters are optional. Each person counts once; the album artist and sample-only credits are excluded. Clues use the edition listed in the answer credits.

    Your scores stay here

    Progress, preferences, and streaks are stored in this browser. Best streaks are personal records, across genres and settings.
